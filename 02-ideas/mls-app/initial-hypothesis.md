# Project: MLS Listing Automation (The "Fast Listing" Tool)

**Date:** 2026-04-19
**Project Status:** Graduated to Planning/Discovery

---

## 1. The Core Idea
A unified software platform for real estate agents and office admins that solves the fragmented, manual process of moving a property from "Contract Signed" to "Active on MLS."

## 2. The Problem
Realtors experience "actual rage" over two main bottlenecks:
1. **Manual Data Entry:** Typing 50+ fields into clunky MLS portals (FlexMLS) which is tedious and error-prone.
2. **Media Management:** The nightmare of receiving 50-100 high-res photos from a photographer, resizing them to meet MLS limits, renaming them, and manually ordering them for the listing.

## 3. The Solution
- **The Collaboration Hook:** Realtors send an upload link to photographers; the platform auto-processes (resizes/orders) the media.
- **The Sidecar UI:** A persistent Chrome Side Panel that allows agents to "One-Click Fill" the MLS form using AI-extracted property data and descriptions.
- **Unified Workflow:** Scheduling, payments, media, and MLS entry in one tab.

## 4. The Wedge
Starting specifically with **Michigan FlexMLS** users to provide a deep, pixel-perfect integration that broad national tools miss.
