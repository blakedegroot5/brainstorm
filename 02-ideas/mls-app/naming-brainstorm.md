# MLS App Naming Brainstorm & Decision Log

**Status:** Deferred (2026-04-19)
**Goal:** Identify a brandable, scaleable name that fits the "Michigan-First" wedge but allows for national expansion.

---

### **Top Candidates**

| Name | Vibe | Pros | Cons |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sidecar** | Integration | Descriptive of the UI; friendly "helper" feel. | Generic; high SEO competition; many existing apps. |
| **Hitch** | Rugged Tool | 1-syllable; fits "Wildlife/Land" brand; strong "Utility" feel. | None identified yet. |
| **ListLogic** | Modern SaaS | Sounds intelligent and automated. | **High Risk:** Domain is $10k+; existing trademark conflicts. |
| **MittenList** | Regional | Instant trust in Michigan market. | Hard to scale outside of Michigan. |
| **AutoLyst** | Tech-Forward | Very "SaaS" feeling; suggests automation/catalyst. | None identified yet. |
| **DashListing** | Speed | Suggests a quick "dash" to get the listing live. | None identified yet. |

---

### **Naming Decisions & Strategy**

*   **2026-04-19:** **Decision to Defer.** Naming is not the primary blocker for MVP development or discovery calls. Internal working name remains **"MLS App Sidecar"** or **"Sidecar"** for now.
*   **Domain Strategy:** Avoid high-premium domains ($1k+). Focus on `.com` availability or modern extensions (`.io`, `.app`) for under $100/year.
*   **Trademark Strategy:** Avoid "Swift" names (Taylor Swift legal risk) and "Logic" names (existing conflicts).
