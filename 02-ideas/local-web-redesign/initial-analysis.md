# Local SMB Web Redesign & Hosting Consulting

**Date:** 2026-04-19
**Status:** Candidate / Under Review

---

## 1. The Hypothesis
There is a massive "Tail" of local Michigan small-to-medium businesses (SMBs) running on outdated, non-responsive, or overpriced legacy web platforms. By using automated "scoping" tools to identify these low-hanging fruit (e.g., slow load times, lack of SSL, non-mobile friendly), we can offer a high-margin redesign + hosting optimization service.

## 2. The "Michigan-First" Wedge
*   **The MEDC Grant Play (Industry 4.0):** The Michigan Economic Development Corporation offers a **50% reimbursement (up to $25k)** for digital transformation in manufacturing. This is our "Trojan Horse" for high-margin projects.
*   **Geographic Trust:** Local business owners prefer working with someone they can "grab a coffee with" in Grand Rapids, Detroit, or Lansing.
*   **Compliance-First:** Focusing on HIPAA (Medical) or MLCC (Liquor) compliance where generic builders fail.

## 3. The Workflow (Presence-First Strategy)
1.  **Lead Identification:** Focus on "No-Website" shops in Muskegon, Holland, and Grand Rapids.
2.  **The "Heritage" Pitch:** Focus on the company's established reputation. "Your shop floor is world-class; your digital presence should match."
3.  **Rapid Implementation:** Deploy professional, responsive 5-page sites (Home, Services, Equipment List, About, Contact).
4.  **The Upsell:** Once a relationship is built, pivot to the **MEDC Grant** and **B2B Portal** features for Phase 2.

## 4. SWOT Analysis

### **Strengths**
*   **Low Barrier to Entry:** Can start with zero capital.
*   **Immediate Cashflow:** Project fees can fund the "Build" phase of the MLS App.
*   **Relationship Building:** Opens doors for future "Software" products once trust is established.

### **Weaknesses**
*   **Service-Heavy:** It is "Work for Hire" (hours for dollars) unless we productize the maintenance/hosting.
*   **High Competition:** Everyone and their cousin is a "web designer." Differentiation is hard without a specific niche.
*   **Scalability:** Hard to scale without hiring designers/project managers.

### **Opportunities**
*   **The "AI Efficiency" Play:** Use AI to handle 80% of the copywriting and layout generation, making a $3k site take 5 hours instead of 40.
*   **Subscription Model:** Offer "Web-as-a-Service" ($200/mo for site + updates + hosting) to build MRR.

### **Threats**
*   **DIY Tools:** Squarespace/Wix are "good enough" for many.
*   **Market Saturation:** Many local businesses are bombarded with these calls.

---

## 5. Alignment with "Fast Cashflow"
*   **Pros:** High upfront payments ($2k - $10k per project).
*   **Cons:** High "Founder Time" requirement. Might distract from the MLS App which has higher long-term "Software" leverage.
