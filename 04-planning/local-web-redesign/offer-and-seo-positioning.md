# Local Web Redesign Offer + SEO Positioning

Date: 2026-04-20
Status: Working strategy doc

## Core concept

Sell a practical "digital presence upgrade" for local Michigan SMBs:
- modern, fast website,
- professional photography content,
- managed hosting/maintenance,
- local SEO foundation.

Primary promise is conversion and credibility, not vanity rankings.

## Positioning

### Primary pitch
"We build a fast, modern site that helps you look credible and generate more qualified calls."

### SEO pitch (supporting, not primary)
- Include local SEO fundamentals in every build.
- Frame SEO as risk reduction and discoverability baseline.
- Avoid ranking guarantees.

## Offer structure

### Tier 1: Build + Launch (one-time)
- Site rebuild (5-page baseline)
- Performance/mobile optimization
- Conversion-focused CTAs and forms
- Basic analytics + call/email tracking setup
- Local SEO foundation:
  - on-page metadata
  - schema basics
  - sitemap/indexing setup
  - Google Business Profile alignment

### Tier 2: Managed Site (monthly)
- Hosting + uptime management
- Security/SSL maintenance
- Minor content updates
- Performance checks
- Monthly reporting snapshot

### Tier 3: SEO Growth Add-on (optional)
- New location/service pages
- GBP posting cadence
- Citation cleanup
- Technical fixes as needed

## Messaging guardrails

Do say:
- "We improve site speed, trust, and qualified inbound opportunities."
- "We provide local SEO best-practice setup and monthly growth options."

Do not say:
- "We guarantee #1 rankings."
- "SEO alone will generate all your leads."

## Why this model fits current strategy

- Fast cashflow via upfront project fees.
- Recurring revenue via managed hosting.
- SEO as a measured upsell, not a risky core promise.
- Keeps delivery predictable and avoids overcommitting to long-horizon ranking outcomes.

## Suggested pricing framework (to validate in discovery)

- Build + Launch: $2k-$5k (niche and complexity dependent)
- Managed Site: $150-$300/mo
- SEO Growth Add-on: priced separately by scope

## Discovery questions to validate before scaling

1. Is owner expectation one-time build or ongoing support?
2. Is monthly managed fee acceptable at $150-$300?
3. Do they value "better calls" more than ranking language?
4. What updates do they actually need month to month?
