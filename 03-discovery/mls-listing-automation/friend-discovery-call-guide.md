# Friend Discovery Call Guide — Real Estate MLS Listing Automation

**Goal:** Understand the exact pain, validate WTP, and test a specific product hypothesis.

**Duration:** 20-30 minutes

**Tone:** Conversational discovery, NOT a pitch. You're learning, not selling or offering equity.

**Important context:** Friend has previously tried to claim 50/50 equity on earlier ideas (Landtrack parcel/life-event tracking) based on "it was my idea." This is discovery validation, not a partnership conversation. Be clear on that boundary if he asks.

---

## Part 1: Confirm the Pain (5 min)

**Opening:**
"I'm exploring an idea around making MLS listing posting less of a slog. From what you said before, manual data entry is a pain. Walk me through what that actually looks like."

**Listen for:**
- How long does posting a listing take? (5 min? 30 min? 1 hour?)
- Which part is slowest? (Photography, typing details, writing descriptions, uploading?)
- How many listings per month do you post?
- What would it be worth to cut that time in half?

---

## Part 2: Competitive Landscape (5 min)

**Question:**
"Have you ever tried tools like Writor (generates descriptions), Cotality's AI features, or Restb.ai (extracts data from photos)? If yes, what frustrated you about them?"

**Listen for:**
- Why didn't they stick with a tool?
- Price too high? UX too clunky? Didn't work well enough? Disconnected from MLS workflow?
- If they haven't tried any: why? (Cost, didn't know they existed, skeptical it would help?)

---

## Part 3: Test the Product Hypothesis (10 min)

**The Pitch (keep it simple):**
"What if there was a tool where you could input property details in any way that's easiest for you—dictate it, type it, upload photos, paste old descriptions—and it automatically filled out your MLS form for you? Not a separate tool you have to copy/paste from. Just direct auto-fill into FlexMLS."

**Questions:**
1. "Would that solve your problem?"
2. "What would that be worth to you monthly? ($20? $50? $100?)"
3. "What would you still want to change about it?"

**Listen for:**
- Is dictation actually valuable to him, or is he indifferent?
- Does he care about the auto-fill part, or would a simpler form be enough?
- Is price a barrier?
- What's the ONE thing he'd most want from a tool like this?

---

## Part 4: Logistics & Next Steps (5 min)

**If positive signal:**
"If I built something like this, would you be willing to test it? Help me refine it?"

**If uncertain:**
"What would need to be true for you to use a tool like this?"

**Before you go:**
- Ask for referrals: "Do you know other agents struggling with this same thing?"
- Permission to follow up: "Can I reach out in a few weeks to see if this is ready?"

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

- If he says "meh, it's not that painful," the pain might be weaker than you think
- If he says "I'd rather have [completely different solution]," you might be solving the wrong problem
- If he asks about features you hadn't planned (video upload, CRM integration, etc.), note it but don't commit

---

## Green Lights to Look For

- "Yeah, that would save me 30 minutes per listing"
- "I'd pay $40/month for that"
- "I've wanted something like this for years"
- He asks detailed questions about how it works (sign of genuine interest)
- He mentions other agents who'd want it
