## 2. Secondary Niche: Specialized Medical & Dental
*Target: Independent Orthodontists, Physical Therapy groups, Private Specialty Clinics.*

### **The "Wedge": HIPAA Compliance & Liability**
*   **The Hook:** Managed HIPAA-compliant infrastructure.
*   **The Value:** Removing the practitioners' legal liability for data breaches.
*   **The Pitch:** "Stop using insecure forms. We provide a HIPAA-compliant 'Patient Portal' and secure hosting that protects your practice and your patients' data."

### **Technical Needs**
*   **BAA Agreements:** Required for hosting providers.
*   **Encrypted Forms:** Secure patient intake.
*   **ADA Accessibility:** Legal requirement for medical facilities.

## 3. Tertiary Niche: Agritourism (Wineries & Breweries)
*Target: Traverse City Wineries, Grand Rapids Breweries, U-Pick Orchards.*

### **The "Wedge": Branding & E-Commerce Compliance**
*   **The Hook:** High-performance, visual-first sites with liquor-compliant e-commerce.
*   **The Value:** Matching the digital aesthetic to the high-end physical experience.
*   **The Pitch:** "Your vineyard is world-class; your website shouldn't look like a template. We build high-speed, visual-first sites that handle seasonal spikes and MLCC-compliant shipping."

### **Technical Needs**
*   **High-Res Media Optimization:** Loading 4K imagery fast on rural 4G/5G.
*   **Compliance Integrations:** VineSpring, Commerce7, or similar alcohol-specific POS/E-comm.
*   **Event Booking:** Integrated tasting room and tour reservations.
