## 2. Technical Infrastructure (The Hosting Strategy)

To maximize margins and minimize "support tickets," we will use a **Modern Static Architecture**.

| Component | Provider | Why? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Framework** | Astro or Next.js | Fastest load times; great for SEO and industrial "Equipment Lists." |
| **Hosting** | Vercel or Netlify | $0-$20 cost to us; highly secure; global CDN. |
| **CMS** | Contentful or Sanity | (Optional) Allows the client to update their own "Equipment List" without touching code. |
| **Domains** | Porkbun / Cloudflare | Clean UI; transparent pricing; no upsell spam. |
| **Email** | Google Workspace | The industry standard; easy to hand off to the client. |

### **The Revenue Model (Recurring)**
*   **"Managed Infrastructure" Fee:** $99/month.
    *   Includes: High-speed hosting, SSL certificate, automated backups, and 30 mins of monthly text/photo updates.
    *   **Net Margin:** ~$80+/month per client.
