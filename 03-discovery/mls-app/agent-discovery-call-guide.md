# Agent Discovery Call Guide (FlexMLS / MichRIC)

**Goal:** Move from "Do you like this idea?" to "How much of a headache is this *exactly*?"

---

## 1. The "Current State" (Manual Audit)
*   "Walk me through the last listing you posted. From the moment the photographer sent you the link to the moment it went 'Active' in Flexmls—what were the steps?"
*   "How long did that take? (Don't guess, try to remember the actual time spent at the desk)."
*   "Who actually does the typing? You? An admin? A Virtual Assistant?"

---

## 2. The "Media Dance" (The Rage Test)
*   "When you get photos from the photographer, do you ever have to resize them or convert them from your phone (HEIC) to get Flexmls to accept them?"
*   "In Flexmls, you have to save a listing as 'Incomplete' before you can even upload photos. Is that true for your workflow? How annoying is that 'double-save' process?"
*   "Have you ever had Zillow or Realtor.com pull the wrong 'Cover Photo' because the Flexmls re-ordering tool was acting jumpy?"
*   **Target Pain:** Photo resizing, re-ordering, and the "Incomplete" workaround.

---

## 3. The "Data Entry Grind" (Accuracy & Compliance)
*   "Which fields in the MichRIC form do you have to look up elsewhere? (e.g., pulling tax records for SEV, looking up school districts, or guessing on the 'Basement Type'?) "
*   "Are you worried about the **Jan 2026 change** where they are removing 'Area' and 'Sub-Area' numbers? How are you planning to handle the new mapping requirements?"
*   "How many 'Compliance Fines' have you or your office paid in the last year for silly typos or wrong category selections?"
*   **Target Pain:** MichRIC-specific field complexity and "Mapping Gap" fears.

---

## 4. The "Solution Reveal" (Hybrid Sidecar vs. Platform)
*   "Most tools (like Ocusell) want you to leave Flexmls and use their dashboard. I’m building a **Chrome Extension** that sits *inside* Flexmls as a side-panel and auto-fills the boxes for you while you're looking at the form. Which approach sounds better to you?"
*   "If an app could 'Look' at your photos and notes and auto-fill the **Top 50 most annoying fields** (Basement type, Sewer/Water, Utilities, etc.) with 90% accuracy, would you trust it, or would you still want to check every box?"
*   **Target Choice:** Validate "Extension-First" (Zero Friction) over "Platform-First" (New Dashboard).

---

## 5. The "ROI & Workflow" (The Money Question)
*   "If I could save you 45-60 minutes per listing and handle all the photo optimization/ordering for you, what is that worth? Is it a $20/listing 'Convenience Fee' or a $50/mo subscription?"
*   "What is the biggest headache in paying your vendors? (Photographers, cleaners, stagers). Do you wish that was part of the listing workflow, or is Venmo/Check 'good enough'?"
*   **Target Signal:** Willingness to pay (WTP) and appetite for integrated payments.

---

## 6. Closing / Referral
*   "Who is the best Transaction Coordinator (TC) or admin you know in Michigan? I'd love to show them this side-panel and see if it solves their biggest headache."
