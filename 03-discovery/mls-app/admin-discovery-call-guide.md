# Admin Discovery Call Guide — MLS Prep-to-Post Workflow

**Goal:** Map the real office-admin workflow, identify bottlenecks, and validate whether a workflow tool is worth paying for.

**Duration:** 25-30 minutes

**Tone:** Workflow discovery only. No feature pitch until the end.

---

## Part 1: Last Listing Walkthrough (10 min)

**Opening:**
"Can you walk me through the last listing you handled, from agreement signed to active on MLS?"

**Ask:**
- "What are the exact steps, in order?"
- "Which tools are used at each step?"
- "Where are handoffs to agents, photographers, or other vendors?"
- "Where do delays usually happen?"

**Capture:**
- Step-by-step sequence
- Tool stack
- Handoff points
- Delay points

---

## Part 2: Pain, Frequency, and Impact (8 min)

**Ask:**
- "Which step is most frustrating or risky?"
- "How many listings do you process per month in busy vs slow season?"
- "How much time does this take per listing?"
- "What mistakes or rework happen most often?"
- "What are the consequences of delays or errors?"

**Capture:**
- Top bottleneck
- Monthly frequency
- Time impact
- Error/rework costs

---

## Part 3: Vendors, Payments, and Coordination (6 min)

**Ask:**
- "How do you schedule photographers/other vendors today?"
- "What breaks in scheduling and delivery?"
- "How are invoices received and paid now?"
- "What is painful about payment tracking or reconciliation?"

**Capture:**
- Scheduling process
- Payment process
- Missing visibility
- Manual work burden

---

## Part 4: Current Alternatives and Spend (4 min)

**Ask:**
- "What software or process have you tried to fix this?"
- "What do you pay for now (tools + time + people)?"
- "Why hasn't the current setup solved it?"

**Capture:**
- Current stack
- Spend signals
- Gaps in current tools

---

## Part 5: Lightweight Concept Test (2 min)

"If a tool reduced your manual listing prep and made handoffs cleaner without changing your MLS workflow, what would need to be true for you to trust it?"

**Optional:**
- "Would you test this on one listing if setup was light?"

---

## Close (1 min)

- "Who else in your office/network has this same workflow pain?"
- "Can I follow up after I compare notes across interviews?"
