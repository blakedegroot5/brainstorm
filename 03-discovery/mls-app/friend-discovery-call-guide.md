# Friend Discovery Call Guide — Real Estate MLS Listing Automation

**Goal:** Understand real workflow pain and buying signals before testing any solution concept.

**Duration:** 20-30 minutes

**Tone:** Conversational discovery, not a pitch.

**Important context:** Friend has previously tried to claim 50/50 equity on earlier ideas. This is customer discovery only, not partnership discussion.

---

## Call Structure (Workflow First)

### Part 1: Last-Listing Walkthrough (8-10 min)

**Opening:**
"Can you walk me through your most recent listing from signed agreement to 'active on MLS'?"

**Ask:**
- "What exact steps happened, in order?"
- "Which tools did you use at each step?"
- "Where did you switch tabs/systems the most?"
- "Where did it slow down or break?"

**Capture:**
- Sequence of steps
- Tool handoffs
- Failure points

---

### Part 2: Pain + Frequency + Cost (6-8 min)

**Ask:**
- "Which step is most annoying or risky?"
- "How many listings like this per month?"
- "How much time does this cost per listing?"
- "What happens when that step goes wrong?"
- "Who feels this pain most: you, admin, or both?"

**Capture:**
- Top pain point
- Frequency per month
- Time/cost impact
- Consequences of errors/delays

---

### Part 3: Current Fixes and Spend (4-5 min)

**Ask:**
- "What do you use today to reduce this pain?"
- "What have you tried that did not stick?"
- "What are you paying for now (tools, staff time, contractors)?"
- "What do those tools still fail to solve?"

**Capture:**
- Existing alternatives
- Why they fail
- Actual spend behavior

---

### Part 4: Concept Test (2-4 min, only after discovery)

**Keep this short:**
"If a tool reduced manual MLS prep work and photo handling without changing your core workflow, what would have to be true for you to trust it?"

**Optional follow-up:**
- "Would you test a lightweight version if it saved meaningful time on one listing?"

**Avoid:**
- Feature dump
- Detailed roadmap discussion
- Early pricing anchors (like $20/$50/$100)

---

### Part 5: Close (1-2 min)

**Ask:**
- "Who else should I talk to that has this exact workflow pain?"
- "Can I follow up after I synthesize what I heard across interviews?"

---

## Red Flags

- "This is minor; not worth fixing"
- "I already solved this fully"
- No time or cost impact
- No willingness to test anything

## Green Lights

- Clear step-by-step pain with repeated frequency
- Quantified time drain per listing
- Existing spend + dissatisfaction
- Referral to other agents/admins with same issue
